Everything in one tidy space

Intention brings your timer, schedule, and reflection together so your day stays on track.

  • Pomodoro Timer

    Work, short, and long breaks with customizable durations and an eight-session cycle. A sound and notification mark the end of each session.

  • Focus Mode

    Turns your entire screen greyscale to kill visual temptation. Pick the window to keep in color; color returns only when it is active. Desktop

  • Scheduler

    A flexible day calendar: drag to create and resize time blocks, assign to-dos, priorities, and breaks. Keep your My 5 Priorities and a to-do list with a Completed archive.

  • Distraction Tracking & Reflection

    A quick-tap counter plus an end-of-session timeline to tag each distraction, and a short reflection check-in after every four sessions.

  • History Heatmap

    A monthly heatmap of focus sessions per day. Click any day for its sessions, distraction breakdown, and planned schedule.

  • Articles

    A small built-in library. Highlight lines to rotate as inspiration on your Timer tab.

  • Accounts Optional

    Sign in with a passwordless email one-time code to unlock the Online area — the home for upcoming features like calendar import, live status, and group Pomodoros. Everything else works signed out.

How it works

  1. 1

    Plan the day

    Set your My 5 Priorities and drag time blocks onto the scheduler.

  2. 2

    Start a focus session

    Run the Pomodoro timer and let the work/break cycle keep you moving.

  3. 3

    Log distractions

    Tap the counter when your mind wanders, then tag each one afterward.

  4. 4

    Review your history

    Check the heatmap to see your sessions, distractions, and planned days.

A few features are desktop-only

The web app covers your timer, scheduler, calendar, history, reading, and sign-in. These features need the desktop build:

  • System-wide greyscale Focus Mode
  • The floating mini-timer overlay
  • The iOS Focus Companion pairing
